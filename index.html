connect google classroom

Connect a Google Cloud OAuth client to pull your coursework and grades from Google Classroom. This is a one-time setup — see Clearancer's setup guide for how to create the client ID and secret. After connecting, Clearancer signs you in with Google, finds your classes, matches each one to a subject tab by name, and imports its coursework as LAS entries (unrelated classes are skipped automatically).
